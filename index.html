Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is Trackability?+

Trackability is a personal progress platform designed to bring study, focus, tasks, tests and other daily tracking into one place.

Can I use it for exams other than NEET-PG?+

Yes. Trackability is designed to be flexible enough for different exams and personal study systems rather than being limited to one examination.

What can I track?+

Depending on the current app version, you can track study sessions, subjects and chapters, focus sessions, tasks, MCQs, tests and personal metrics such as gym, weight, nutrition and sleep.

Is Trackability available on mobile?+

Trackability is being developed with mobile use in mind. Use the available Android download or the current web app experience from the Get Started section.

Is my data synced?+

Account-based synchronization is part of the Trackability architecture. Availability of particular sync features depends on the current version of the application.

How can I report a problem?+

Use the Support / Contact section below. Include your device, what you were doing, and what happened so the issue can be investigated more efficiently.

About Trackability

Built around one simple idea.

Progress is easier to understand when you can see it. Trackability is being built as a flexible system for turning everyday work and habits into something you can record, review and improve.

What began around study tracking can grow into a broader personal progress platform — without losing the simplicity of knowing what to do next.
