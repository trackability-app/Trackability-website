Features

Everything connected around your preparation.

Trackability combines planning, focused work, academic practice and personal tracking without forcing you into a single rigid workflow.

01

Home & Dashboard

See your day, MCQs, tasks and progress in one place.

02

Tasks & Time Blocks

Create tasks, assign dates and times, repeat them, reschedule them and connect them to subjects or chapters.

03

Focus & Pomodoro

Study, Revision or Other sessions with presets, custom durations, fullscreen timer, Pomodoro and history.

04

Syllabus & Revision Hub

Organise subjects and chapters, track completion and send revision topics directly into tasks.

05

MCQ Hub

Collect chapter-level MCQ topics and move them into your daily plan.

06

MCQ ID Tracker

Keep a personal repository of question IDs, concepts, traps, notes and sources for later review.

07

Grand Tests

Record GT name, platform, correct, wrong and left; calculate scores and visualise trends.

08

Mini Tests & MCQ History

Log mini tests, Q-bank practice and daily MCQ totals with monthly history.

09

Mistake Book

Keep recurring errors and high-yield learning points visible for revision.

10

Gym Tracker

Log workouts, body weight and historical sessions.

11

Sleep Tracker

Record sleep duration, quality bands, monthly totals and daily trends.

12

Protein & Calories

Log daily intake and review monthly totals and averages.

13

Statistics & History

Understand study hours, focus streaks, MCQs, GT performance, revision and personal trends.

14

Custom Marking Schemes

Adapt test scoring such as +4/-1, +1/0 or +1/-0.25 rather than being locked to one exam.

15

Sync & Account

Email-based account access and Supabase-backed cloud sync for supported data.
