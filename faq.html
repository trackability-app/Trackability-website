FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Here are the common questions about the product, data, exams and tracking features.

What is Trackability?

Trackability is a study, productivity and personal-tracking workspace designed to connect planning, focused work, academic practice and routine tracking.

Which exams does it support?

The current workflows include NEET PG and NEET UG. The system is designed to be adaptable with custom subjects, chapters and marking schemes.

How are Grand Tests scored?

The app can use a configurable marking scheme. Examples include +4/-1, +1/0 and +1/-0.25. Do not assume a single fixed scheme for every exam.

Can I track more than study?

Yes. Trackability includes gym, sleep, protein and calorie tracking alongside academic and productivity features.

Does Trackability provide medical advice?

No. Fitness, sleep and nutrition features are tracking tools only and are not medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

Does Trackability guarantee an exam result?

No. Trackability does not guarantee marks, rank, percentile, admission, college, qualification or career outcomes.

Can my data be synced?

Authenticated account data may sync to cloud infrastructure using Supabase. Some information may also be stored locally/browser-side as described in the Privacy Policy.

How do I contact Trackability?

Email trackability.app@gmail.com for support, feedback or questions.
