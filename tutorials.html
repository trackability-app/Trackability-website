Tutorials

Learn Trackability feature by feature.

Step-by-step written guides. No video required. Each guide explains what to tap, what the feature records and where the result appears.

Getting StartedPlanningFocusStudy & RevisionMCQs & TestsPersonal TrackingStatistics

Getting Started

1

Open the working app and choose the exam/preparation context you want to track.

2

Set up your basic profile and preferences, then begin from the Home dashboard.

Planning: Tasks & Time Blocks

1

Open Tasks and create a task with its subject or miscellaneous category.

2

Assign the date and time span. Use repeating tasks when the same block recurs.

3

Reschedule or edit the task when your real day changes. Use notifications where enabled.

Focus Timer & Pomodoro

1

Choose Study, Revision or Other and optionally attach a subject/chapter.

2

Select 25, 50, 90 minutes or a custom duration.

3

For Pomodoro, configure work duration, break duration and session count. Completed sessions flow into Focus Log and History.

Syllabus, Revision Hub & Mistake Book

1

Open Syllabus and select a subject to view chapters and completion state.

2

Send important chapters into Revision Hub or MCQ Hub depending on the work you want to do next.

3

Record recurring errors and high-yield traps in the Mistake Book for targeted revision.

MCQ ID Tracker, Mini Tests & Grand Tests

1

Use MCQ ID Tracker for individual question references: ID, subject, chapter/topic, concept, note, source and date.

2

Use Mini Test for test-level practice and ensure its questions contribute to the appropriate MCQ history.

3

For a Grand Test, record test name, platform, correct, wrong and left. The score is calculated using your configured marking scheme.

Gym, Sleep, Protein & Calories

1

Open the relevant tracker and choose the date.

2

Log the session or intake and save it to the historical ledger.

3

Use monthly totals, averages and graphs to see patterns rather than relying on memory.

Statistics & History

1

Review study hours, focus streaks, MCQs, Grand Tests and revision activity.

2

Compare historical periods and identify where consistency or performance is changing.

3

Use the pattern to adjust your next day instead of guessing what went wrong.
